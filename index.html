Frequently asked questions

Common Questions.

How do I know if LD Industry is interested in my property?

Share your property details below. Our team can review the information and determine whether it meets our current acquisition criteria.

Am I obligated to sell if I submit my property?

No. Submitting an inquiry is only a request for review and does not create an agreement to sell.

How is my land evaluated?

Potential considerations include location, acreage, access, zoning, market conditions, and development potential.

How quickly can I receive an offer?

Review times vary by property. If the property may be a fit, the team will contact you with next steps and an expected timeline.
